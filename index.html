Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your PDF is opened and processed inside your browser, on your own device. Paperbench has no server that receives files.

Is it really free?

Yes. All six tools are free with no daily limit and no account. Free handles one file at a time (Merge takes as many as you like).

What does Pro add?

Four extra tools (Organize pages, OCR for scanned PDFs, Watermark and Page numbers) plus batch processing: drop many PDFs into Compress, Rotate or PDF to images and get them all back in one zip.

How do I activate Pro?

After you pay, you get a license key by email. Click Go Pro, paste the key and choose Activate. One key works in up to 3 browsers.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Once you cancel, you won't be charged again.

Will compressing change my document?

Light compression keeps everything as it is. Strong and Extreme turn each page into an image, which makes files much smaller but means text can no longer be selected or searched.

How big a file can I use?

There's no fixed limit. Because the work happens on your device, very large files depend on how much memory it has and may take longer.

Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Your files

Your PDFs are opened and processed in your browser, on your device. They are never sent to Paperbench or to anyone else. There is no Paperbench server that receives files.

Accounts, cookies and tracking

Paperbench has no accounts, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

What is stored in your browser

If you activate Pro, your license key is saved in this browser so you stay signed in to Pro. Choosing "Remove from this browser" deletes it.

Other services involved

  • Lemon Squeezy handles payments and license keys. When you activate Pro, and about once a day afterwards, your browser sends your license key to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is still valid. Paperbench never sees your card details.
  • Code libraries and the font are loaded from the public networks cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts. As with any web request, they receive your IP address and browser type. They never receive your files.
  • Our web host serves the site's pages and may keep standard access logs.
